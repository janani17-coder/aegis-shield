# Policy 6: Incident Response

**Policy ID:** POL-06  
**Owner:** Security Team  
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Purpose

To ensure that suspected security incidents are reported, assessed, contained, investigated, and resolved in a consistent manner.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Aura Workforce employees, contractors, administrators, applications, infrastructure, and third parties handling its information.

## 3. Rules

1. Users must report suspected data exposure, account compromise, unauthorized access, malware, and unexplained security changes through the approved incident-reporting channel as soon as possible.
2. The receiving team must record each reported incident with a unique identifier, report time, reporter details when available, affected systems, and a description of the concern.
3. The designated incident responder must assess the severity and potential impact of each report and document the initial assessment.
4. Suspected compromised accounts must be restricted or secured promptly according to the incident response procedure.
5. Incident responders must preserve relevant logs, timestamps, system records, and other evidence while maintaining a record of evidence handling.
6. Responders must document significant actions, decisions, responsible personnel, and timestamps in the incident record.
7. Suspected exposure of payroll or Restricted employee data must be escalated to the Security Team and relevant data owner.
8. External communications and notifications must be approved by authorized personnel and completed within applicable legal and contractual deadlines.
9. An incident must not be closed until its resolution, remaining risks, and required follow-up actions are documented.
10. The incident owner must record lessons learned and assign corrective actions after significant incidents.

## 4. Roles

- **All users:** Report suspected incidents promptly.
- **Incident responders:** Assess, investigate, contain, and document incidents.
- **Security Team:** Coordinate response and determine security escalation.
- **System and data owners:** Support recovery and assess business or data impact.
- **Management:** Approve major business decisions and required external communications.

## 5. Exceptions

Any deviation from the response procedure must be documented with its reason, approving authority, risks, and follow-up actions. Emergency actions may be taken to reduce immediate harm, with decisions recorded as soon as practical.

## Threat Mapping

Supports STRIDE threats **T-03, T-09, T-11, T-12, T-19, and T-28** by providing a documented process for investigating unauthorized access, tampering, and data exposure.
