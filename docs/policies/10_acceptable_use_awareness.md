# Policy 10: Acceptable Use & Security Awareness

**Policy ID:** POL-10  
**Owner:** HR and Security Team  
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Purpose

To define acceptable use of Aura Workforce systems and ensure that users understand their responsibility to protect company information.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, administrators, and other authorized users of Aura Workforce accounts, devices, applications, networks, and information.

## 3. Rules

1. Users must use company systems and information only for authorized business purposes and permitted personal use, where applicable.
2. Users must not share accounts, passwords, authentication codes, or recovery codes with other people.
3. Users must lock their screens when leaving an unattended workstation and protect devices from unauthorized use.
4. Users must not install unapproved software or connect unauthorized devices to company systems without the required approval.
5. Users must verify unexpected requests for sensitive data, payments, credentials, or account changes before acting on them.
6. Users must not send Confidential or Restricted information through unapproved email, messaging, file-sharing, or AI services.
7. Users must report suspected phishing, malware, lost devices, accidental data sharing, and suspicious account activity through the approved reporting channel.
8. All users must complete assigned security awareness training at onboarding and at least annually thereafter.
9. Training completion must be recorded, and overdue training must be reported to the responsible manager.
10. Violations of this policy must be documented and reviewed under the organization's approved disciplinary and incident-management processes.

## 4. Roles

- **All users:** Follow acceptable-use rules and report security concerns.
- **HR:** Coordinates onboarding and training records.
- **Security Team:** Provides awareness material and reporting guidance.
- **Managers:** Ensure team members complete required training.
- **IT administrators:** Apply approved technical restrictions.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions require written approval from the relevant manager and Security Team, a documented reason, compensating controls, and an expiry date. Exceptions must not violate legal or contractual obligations.

## Threat Mapping

Supports STRIDE threats **T-03, T-08, T-19, and T-29** by promoting secure account use, responsible data handling, and awareness of unauthorized access risks.
