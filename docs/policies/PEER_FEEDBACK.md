
# Peer Review Feedback — Security Policy Pack

## Reviewer 1 — Overall Feedback

### Clarity and Structure
Both policies are short, well-written, and easy to navigate. The Purpose, Scope, Rules, Roles, and Exceptions sections make the policies clear and organized.

### Alignment with Security Standards
The reviewer noted that the policies align with modern cybersecurity practices, including:
- Passwords of at least 15 characters.
- Avoiding mandatory password resets every 90 days.
- Enforcing multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Removing access for offboarded users within one business day.

### Suggested Improvement
Some terms, such as "checked on the server," "service accounts," and "STRIDE threats," may be difficult for general employees to understand. Simpler wording would help if the policies are intended for a general audience.

## Reviewer 2 — Feedback

### Difficult Terms
The reviewer identified these terms as potentially unfamiliar to non-technical users:
- MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)
- Privilege
- Authentication

### Rules That Need Clarification
- Explain when users must complete MFA and which approved verification methods they can use.
- Explain who can access specific information and why.
- Explain what users should do when they cannot follow a rule or encounter a problem.

### Understanding of the MFA Policy
The reviewer understood that users must protect their accounts with an additional verification step beyond a password. Examples include approving a login through an authentication app or another approved method.

## Improvements Identified

1. Explain technical terms in plain English when they first appear.
2. Clarify when MFA is required and provide examples of approved verification methods.
3. Explain access permissions in terms of who can access information and for what purpose.
4. Clearly state how users can report problems or request an exception.
5. Keep the existing short, structured policy format.
