# Task 15: Tabletop Exercise Scenario

## Exercise title
Aegis Shield - Payroll Security Incident

## Exercise type
Spoken incident response tabletop exercise.

## Participants
- Facilitator: Project owner
- Participant: Friend acting as the SOC Analyst
- Optional roles: Incident Response Lead, HR Representative

## Scenario
At 11:30 PM, the security monitoring system generates a PAY-01 Night Salary Access alert.

The alert indicates that an account accessed multiple salary records outside normal business hours.

A short time later, a PAY-02 Full Payroll Export alert appears.

The Payroll System Owner has not yet confirmed whether the activity was authorized.

The team must investigate, protect payroll information, preserve evidence and communicate with the appropriate stakeholders.

## Exercise objectives
1. Practice detecting and confirming a suspected payroll incident.
2. Identify who has authority to approve containment.
3. Practice evidence preservation.
4. Understand internal and external notification responsibilities.
5. Identify weaknesses in the existing incident response playbook.

## Five hard questions
1. What are your first three actions after receiving the alert?
2. How would you confirm whether the payroll export was authorized?
3. Who can approve restricting the suspicious account?
4. Who decides whether affected employees or regulators must be notified?
5. What would you do if the payroll audit logs were incomplete?

## Exercise rules
- Discuss the scenario verbally.
- Do not access real payroll information or production systems.
- Ask the five questions one at a time.
- Record the participant's actual answers.
- Record unclear responsibilities, missing information and response delays.
- Do not invent participant responses or exercise results.

## Expected output
A completed tabletop notes document and an improvements document based on the actual discussion.
