# Staff Security Awareness – Knowledge Check

Test your understanding of basic workplace security.

## Question 1 – Phishing

You receive an unexpected email asking you to verify your company password through a link. What should you do?

- A. Click the link immediately
- B. Reply with your password
- C. Do not click; report the message through the approved channel

**Correct answer: C.** Unexpected password requests should be treated as suspicious.

## Question 2 – Passwords

What is the safest password practice?

- A. Reuse one password everywhere
- B. Use unique passwords and an approved password manager
- C. Share passwords with colleagues

**Correct answer: B.** Unique passwords reduce the impact of a compromised account.

## Question 3 – MFA

You receive an MFA approval request that you did not initiate. What should you do?

- A. Approve it
- B. Ignore the request and report it
- C. Share the verification code

**Correct answer: B.** Unexpected authentication requests should be reported.

## Question 4 – Sensitive information

A colleague requests employee salary information without a clear business reason. What should you do?

- A. Send the spreadsheet
- B. Verify their authorization before sharing anything
- C. Upload it to a personal drive

**Correct answer: B.** Access to confidential information must be authorized.

## Question 5 – Suspicious activity

You notice an unfamiliar login notification on your work account. What should you do?

- A. Ignore it
- B. Report it promptly through the approved security channel
- C. Forward your password to IT

**Correct answer: B.** Prompt reporting helps the security team investigate.
